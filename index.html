About Wishly & Verification Information

Wishly is a benign, privacy-first web utility that empowers anyone to design aesthetic surprise websites for loved ones. Every card is private, confidential, and completely free from ads, telemetry trackers, and spyware.

🔒 Confidential: Only people with your unique generated link can open your keepsake card.
✨ 100% Free & Ad-Free: No subscription traps, no paywalls, no pop-ups, and no spam.
🏢 Publisher: Developed, operated, and maintained with care by AV Infra Tech — Digital Engineering Studio.
